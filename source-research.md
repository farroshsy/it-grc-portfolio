## Use Boundary

All references will be presented with their publication name and version or date where available. The application will use paraphrased, operational mappings rather than reproduce restricted standards. The BI/OJK case study will state that applicability and interpretations are illustrative and require qualified review for an organization’s actual product, license category, and operating model.
